# calendar-sync

calendar-sync is a personal tool that reads my own Google Calendar and shows the events in a desktop dashboard on my laptop. It is used only by its developer and is not offered to other people.

## Privacy Policy

- The app requests read access to the developer's own Google Calendar.
- Calendar data is downloaded to the developer's own computer and stored only there.
- No data is sent to any server, shared with, or sold to any third party.
- Access can be revoked at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

Contact: open an issue at <https://github.com/Efwaz/calendar-sync/issues>.
